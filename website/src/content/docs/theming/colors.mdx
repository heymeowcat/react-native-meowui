title: Color tokens
description: The complete semantic color palette for light and dark mode.
---

The full token set. Mochi Press is oat-cream paper, espresso ink and blueberry
primary, with soft semantic tone pairs (tinted well, tinted border, deep
text) for badges. Every enabled `on*` color pairs with its fill at AA
contrast; `onSurfaceSubtle` is reserved for disabled states and quiet
chrome. Control borders (`outlineStrong`) clear 3:1 against the milk
surface.

Two shadow tokens, both warm and alpha-carrying: `shadow` is the translucent
ink behind tactile controls (buttons, chips, segmented keys, the switch
thumb), `shadowSoft` the quieter paper tone cast beneath in-flow surfaces.
Borders are always 1px.

## Dark — cocoa desk

Warm cocoa ground: containers lift by brightness, borders deepen, the
blueberry primary flips to pale periwinkle with espresso content, and
semantic tones lighten while their wells darken.
