---
title: Accessibility
description: What MeowUI guarantees out of the box, and what stays your responsibility.
---

Accessibility is built into the components rather than bolted on afterwards.
Here is the contract.

## What the kit guarantees

**Roles and states.** Every interactive component sets the right
`accessibilityRole` and `accessibilityState`:

- `Button`, `IconButton`, `FAB`, `Chip` → `button` with `disabled` / `busy` /
  `selected`
- `Checkbox` → `checkbox` with `checked`
- `Radio` → `radio` inside a `radiogroup` container, with `checked`
- `Switch` → `switch` with `checked`
- `Slider` → `adjustable` with `accessibilityValue` (min/max/now) **and**
  `increment` / `decrement` accessibility actions
- `Tabs`, `BottomNavigation` → `tablist` / `tab` with `selected`
- `SegmentedButtons` → `radiogroup` + `radio` (single) or buttons (multi)

**Labels with fallbacks.** Components derive a sensible
`accessibilityLabel` when one exists — `Button` falls back to its string
children, `FAB` to its label, and `Avatar` exposes its label as the
accessible name only when it renders an image. When the visible content is
ambiguous (an icon-only `IconButton`), passing `accessibilityLabel` is on
you, and the JSDoc says so.

**Overlay semantics.** Portaled overlays are focusable and labeled: dialogs
expose their title, scrims are labeled dismiss buttons, collapsed accordion
content is hidden from the accessibility tree, and the portal overlay frame
itself is transparent to assistive tech so portaled content stays reachable.

**Contrast.** Both reference palettes pair every enabled `on*` content color
with its fill at ≥ 4.5:1 (AA) — including muted variants and the dark theme.
`onSurfaceSubtle` is reserved for disabled states and decorative chrome,
which WCAG exempts from the ratio.

## What stays your responsibility

- **Ambiguous icons** — pass `accessibilityLabel` to icon-only controls.
- **Screen-order context** — announce a toast's meaning if the message alone
  doesn't carry it (`AccessibilityInfo.announceForAccessibility`).
- **Touch targets in your own layouts** — kit controls meet minimum sizes via
  `theme.metrics`; if you shrink them with styles, re-check the 44×44 guideline.
- **Localization** — components accept any string; pluralization and RTL text
  direction are yours. (Layouts use logical properties — `marginStart`,
  `paddingStart` — so RTL mirroring works, but full RTL audit coverage is
  ongoing.)
